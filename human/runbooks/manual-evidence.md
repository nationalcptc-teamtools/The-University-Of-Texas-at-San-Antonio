# Manual evidence and result cards

Zerologon, spraying, DoS/crash testing, domain changes, account authorization,
and other uncertain or disruptive work remain manual-only. Use the reviewed
cards in `cptc_harness/resources/MANUAL-ONLY-CARDS.md` and attach a local
human-reviewed evidence reference through the permitted result-intake format.

A candidate or scanner match is not a confirmed vulnerability. Confirmation
requires the actual affected target, a reviewed artifact reference, and human
review. Keep passwords, cookies, tokens, and private response bodies out of
notes, filenames, arguments, and model context.
